## New Features

- The Spell List Manager now prompts to Save, Discard, or Cancel when leaving a list with unsaved spell edits through Back to Lists or by closing the window. Edits that net out against the saved list do not trigger the prompt.

## Bug Fixes

- Custom, granted, and merged lists, and renamed modified copies, keep their own page names instead of the registry name.
- Merged list descriptions and the identifier-mismatch warning now name source lists from the item registry.
- Modified copies of stock spell lists no longer lose the link to their originals. A one-time migration on world load restores links for existing modified copies.
- Renaming a list no longer drops pending spell edits.
- The Spell List Manager and Class Rules spell list picker now name class, subclass, species, and background lists from the system's item registry, matching the Compendium Browser.
