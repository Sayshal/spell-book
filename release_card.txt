## New Features

- Added `syncGrantedSpellList` and `setClassSpellLists` to the module API.
- Species and feat granted spells are mirrored into a per-actor granted spell list.
- The Merge Spell Lists dialog can merge selected lists into an existing merged list.
- The Spell Book has a read-only Granted Spells tab grouped by the species or feat that granted them.
